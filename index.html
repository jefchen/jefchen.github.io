Parlor

A personal assistant, made by Jeff Chen for his own use.

Parlor is a private, single-user assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. With the owner's permission it reads his Google Calendar and Gmail (read-only) so it can answer his questions about his own schedule and history, for example what happened on a given day or when a trip was booked.

It is not a public product: there is no sign-up, and it is used only by its owner with his own Google account.
