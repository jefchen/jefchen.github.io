Privacy policy

Parlor · last updated November 7, 2026

Who uses it

Parlor is a personal assistant used only by its owner, Jeff Chen, with his own Google account. No one else signs in to it.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, Parlor reads his Gmail messages and Google Calendar events, read-only. It does not send, change or delete email or calendar events.

How the data is used

The data is used only to answer the owner's own questions and to keep his personal notes, for example what happened on a day or when a trip was booked. It is processed on the owner's own computer.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train general-purpose AI models. Parts of it may be sent to the AI model provider the owner has chosen, only to answer his own requests.

Storage and deletion

Notes made from the data are kept on the owner's computer. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's security settings (Third-party apps & services), which stops any further access.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Parlor's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
